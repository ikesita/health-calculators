Body Fat Calculator (US Navy Method)

Estimate body fat percentage from tape measurements of your neck, waist and hips.

Reference ranges (adults): Men — 6–13% athlete/fitness, 14–17% average-lean, 18–24% somewhat high, 25%+ high. Women — 14–20% athlete/fitness, 21–24% average-lean, 25–31% somewhat high, 32%+ high.
The Navy method estimates from circumferences and typically carries an error of a few percentage points versus lab methods (DEXA, hydrostatic). Use it to track change over time rather than as an exact figure.

Your input stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. See also the BMI Calculator.
