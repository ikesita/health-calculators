How it works: maximum heart rate is estimated as 220 − age, and each zone boundary is resting HR + (max HR − resting HR) × intensity — the Karvonen, or heart-rate-reserve, method. Because it uses your resting heart rate, the zones shift as your fitness changes. The 220 − age figure is a population average: your true maximum can easily be 10–15 bpm higher or lower, so treat these zones as a starting guide and adjust by how the effort feels. Measure resting heart rate first thing in the morning, lying in bed, for the most consistent value.
