This calculator uses the Epley formula: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). It is an estimate for planning training weights, not a measured max. Accuracy is best with low-rep sets and drops noticeably above about 10 reps, and results vary by exercise, technique and fatigue. Use the percentage table to pick working weights, and always lift with a spotter or safety bars when attempting heavy singles.
