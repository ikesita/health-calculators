Sleep Cycle Calculator

Sleep moves in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle — rather than mid-cycle — often feels easier. Times include about 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Cycle length varies from person to person (roughly 80–120 minutes), and so does the time you take to fall asleep. Treat these times as a starting point to experiment with, not a rule — and if you regularly feel unrefreshed, total sleep time matters more than fine-tuning the alarm by a few minutes. Most adults need 7–9 hours of sleep.
