Blood Alcohol Calculator (BAC)

A rough estimate of blood alcohol content using the Widmark formula.

Never drive after drinking. This calculator gives a very rough estimate only. Your actual blood alcohol level depends on food, body composition, medications, health and how fast you drank, and it can be considerably higher than shown here. Any amount of alcohol impairs driving — a low estimate is never a permission to drive.

How it works: BAC (‰) = alcohol in grams ÷ (body weight in kg × r) − 0.15 ‰ × hours, where r is 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women. The result is shown as a percentage (‰ ÷ 10). Pure alcohol in grams ≈ drink volume (ml) × strength (ABV %) × 0.008 — for example, a 500 ml beer at 5% is about 20 g. Two of those beers is the 40 g default above. The formula assumes the alcohol is fully absorbed instantly, which is not how drinking works in real life, so treat the number as a lower-bound guide, not a measurement.
