Hume: men 0.32810W + 0.33929H − 29.5336, women 0.29569W + 0.41813H − 43.2933. These are population estimates that use no body-composition input, so they can be off by several kilograms for very muscular or very heavy individuals — the implied body fat figure is only as good as the LBM estimate.

Common questions

Lean body mass vs fat-free mass — what's the difference?
Fat-free mass excludes all fat. Lean body mass includes a small amount of essential fat (in cell membranes, bone marrow and the central nervous system), so it is slightly higher. In everyday use the two terms are often swapped.

Why track lean body mass?
When losing weight, the goal is usually to lose fat while keeping lean mass. When gaining, a rising LBM with stable waist size suggests muscle gain rather than fat gain.

How accurate is this?
Height-and-weight formulas typically land within a few kilograms of DEXA for average builds, and worse at the extremes. This page gives general estimates only and is not medical advice.
