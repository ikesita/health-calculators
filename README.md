# Health Calculators

Simple, free health and fitness calculators. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — your input is never uploaded to a server. It covers body composition, calories, running, sleep and pregnancy-related estimates, for general guidance only — not medical advice.

## 🌐 Live site

**[https://ikesita.github.io/health-calculators/](https://ikesita.github.io/health-calculators/)**

## Main tools

- **Body composition**: BMI, Body Fat (US Navy), Ideal Weight, Waist-to-Hip Ratio, Waist-to-Height Ratio, Body Surface Area, Lean Body Mass, FFMI, Somatotype
- **Calories & nutrition**: TDEE & Calorie Calculator, BMR, Macro Calculator, Calorie Deficit, Calories Burned by Activity, Glycemic Index, Sweat Rate
- **Running & fitness**: Running Pace, Race Time Predictor, VO2 Max, Heart Rate Zone, Heart Rate Recovery, One-Rep Max
- **Daily life**: Age Calculator, Sleep Cycle Calculator, Daily Water Intake, Steps to Miles & Calories, Caffeine Half-Life, BAC Estimator, Smoking Cost Calculator
- **Pregnancy & health checks**: Pregnancy Due Date, Ovulation Calculator, Period Calculator, Conception Date, Pregnancy Weight Gain, Blood Pressure Category, eGFR

## How to use

Open the live site in your browser and pick a tool — no installation or sign-up needed.

## Note

These calculators are for general information only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Please consult a healthcare professional for health decisions.
