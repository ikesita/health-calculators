Common questions

What is a normal eGFR?
90 or above is KDIGO category G1 (normal to high); 60–89 is G2 (mildly decreased). eGFR declines gradually with age even in healthy people.

Which formula is used?
The CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation — the current standard recommended by KDIGO and the National Kidney Foundation, developed without a race coefficient.

Does a low eGFR mean kidney disease?
Not by itself. CKD requires persistence over 3+ months and often other markers (such as urine albumin). Repeat testing and medical review are needed.
