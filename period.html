How it works: each predicted period simply adds your average cycle length to the previous start date. If your cycles vary by a few days, treat each date as the middle of a likely window of a few days either side.

Prediction only — please read: real cycles vary from month to month, and stress, travel, illness, medication and many other factors can move a period earlier or later. These dates are a planning estimate, not medical advice. If your period is much later than expected, is frequently irregular, or you have pain or other symptoms that worry you, talk to a healthcare professional.
