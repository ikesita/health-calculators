General reference only: VO2 max ranges in ml/kg/min, simplified from commonly published fitness-category tables. Below "Good" the bands are usually labelled Fair and then Poor; above "Excellent" is Superior.

How to take the test: warm up, then walk one mile (1.61 km) on flat ground as fast as you can — walk, don't run — and take your heart rate immediately at the finish. Enter the time in minutes (15.5 for 15 min 30 sec). The Rockport equation was developed on adults aged 30–69 and is an estimate: a lab test is the only direct measurement, and results vary with course, weather and how the heart rate is taken.
