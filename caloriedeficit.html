The math: 1 kg of body fat stores roughly 7,700 kcal, so losing 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 kg per week needs an average deficit of about 275 / 550 / 1,100 kcal per day. Real results vary — metabolism adapts and not all weight lost is fat. Do not go below generally recommended minimum intakes (commonly cited as about 1,200 kcal/day for women and 1,500 kcal/day for men) without medical supervision, and consult a doctor or registered dietitian before starting a calorie-restricted diet, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or have any medical condition or history of eating disorders.
