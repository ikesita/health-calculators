How it works: ovulation is estimated at 14 days before the next period, so the ovulation day is your last period's first day plus (cycle length − 14). The fertile window covers the 5 days before ovulation (sperm can survive up to about 5 days) through the day after (the egg lives about a day).

Estimate only — please read: this calculator assumes a regular cycle and the textbook 14-day luteal phase. Real ovulation varies from cycle to cycle and person to person, and stress, illness and many other factors can shift it. Do not rely on this tool for contraception or for medical decisions. If you are trying to conceive, have irregular cycles, or have health concerns, talk to a healthcare professional; ovulation test kits and basal body temperature tracking give direct evidence that a calendar cannot.
