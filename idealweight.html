The healthy range is the weight band where BMI falls between 18.5 and 24.9. The Devine formula (1974, originally for drug dosing) estimates ideal weight as 50 kg (men) or 45.5 kg (women) plus 2.3 kg for every inch over 5 feet. Both are rough population guides: they don't account for muscle mass, bone structure or body composition, so an athletic person can be healthy above the range and a person inside it can still have health risks. For personal advice, consult a healthcare professional.
