Common questions

How are calories burned calculated from METs?
Calories per minute = MET × 3.5 × body weight in kg ÷ 200. One MET is your resting energy use; running at 6 mph is 9.8 METs, so it uses about 9.8 times the resting rate.

Why does body weight matter?
Moving a heavier body takes more energy, so the same activity burns more calories at a higher weight.

How accurate is this?
Treat it as a comparison guide between activities. Trackers and formulas can each be off by 10–25% for an individual.
