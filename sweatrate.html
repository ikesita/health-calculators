Weigh in consistent conditions (dry, minimal clothing) for a meaningful result; food eaten during the session and respiratory water loss are not captured.

Common questions

What sweat rates are normal?
Anywhere from ~0.3 L/h in cool, easy conditions to 2 L/h or more in hot, hard exercise. The same person can double their rate between winter and summer — re-test across the season.

Why does 2% matter?
Body-mass losses beyond roughly 2% are where studies consistently find performance dropping and heat strain rising. Small losses are fine and normal.

Should I replace all the salt too?
On sessions over ~1–2 hours, or if your sweat leaves white salt marks, include sodium (sports drink or salty food). Dizziness, confusion, vomiting or a racing pulse in the heat are warning signs — stop, cool down and seek medical help. This page is general information, not medical advice.
