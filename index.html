Health Calculators

Simple, free health and fitness calculators. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — your input is never uploaded to a server.

BMI Calculator
Your Body Mass Index, category and healthy weight range.
Body Fat Calculator
Estimate body fat percentage with the US Navy tape-measure method.
TDEE & Calorie Calculator
Daily calories to maintain, lose or gain weight, from BMR and activity.
Running Pace Calculator
Pace per km and mile, plus predicted 5K to marathon finish times.
Age Calculator
Exact age in years, months and days, and total days lived.
Pregnancy Due Date Calculator
Estimated due date and current week from your last period date.
Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator
Your WHR and the WHO health risk category for women and men.
Daily Water Intake Calculator
A rough daily water target in liters from your weight and activity level.
One-Rep Max Calculator
Estimate your 1RM with the Epley formula, plus a 95–60% weight table.
Macro Calculator
Daily protein, fat and carb grams from your calorie target and split.
These calculators provide general estimates only and are not medical advice. Please consult a healthcare professional for personal health decisions.
