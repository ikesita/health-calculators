Important: This calculator gives estimates only, based on a standard 28-day cycle. Real due dates vary, only a small share of babies arrive on the exact estimated date, and cycle length or conception timing can shift the estimate by days or weeks. Please confirm your due date and pregnancy progress with your doctor, midwife, or another healthcare provider, and follow their guidance over any online calculator.
