Method: a due date is conventionally LMP + 280 days, and ovulation/conception is conventionally LMP + (cycle length − 14) days — day 14 of a 28-day cycle. So from a due date, conception ≈ due date − 266 days (for a 28-day cycle). The fertile window is shown as the 5 days before conception through 1 day after, reflecting sperm survival of up to ~5 days and an egg lifespan of ~24 hours.

Important: This is a rough estimate only. Real ovulation timing varies between people and between cycles, and sperm survival varies too, so the actual conception date can differ by several days. This tool is for general information and cannot establish dates for medical, legal or paternity purposes. Please rely on your healthcare provider's ultrasound dating for anything that matters.

Common questions

How is conception estimated from a due date?
Due date − 266 days. The 266 comes from the standard 280-day pregnancy length minus the ~14 days from the period's start to ovulation in a 28-day cycle.

Can conception happen on a day other than ovulation day?
Fertilisation happens within about a day of ovulation, but intercourse several days before ovulation can still lead to pregnancy because sperm survive up to ~5 days. That is why the window, not just one day, matters.

My cycle is irregular. Can I still use this?
You can, but the estimate becomes much less reliable. With irregular cycles, ovulation may be far from the textbook day; ultrasound dating is the better source.
