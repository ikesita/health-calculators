Common questions

How much weight should I gain during pregnancy?
It depends on your BMI before pregnancy: 12.5–18 kg if underweight, 11.5–16 kg for a healthy weight, 7–11.5 kg if overweight, and 5–9 kg with obesity (IOM guidelines).

How much gain is expected in the first trimester?
About 0.5–2 kg in total across the first trimester; the steadier weekly rate applies from the second trimester onward.

What if I am carrying twins?
The IOM twin guidelines are 16.8–24.5 kg (healthy weight), 14.1–22.7 kg (overweight) and 11.3–19.1 kg (obesity). No guideline was set for twins when underweight.
