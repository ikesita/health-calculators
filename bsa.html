Body surface area (BSA) is used in medicine to scale things like drug doses and metabolic rates to body size.

Mosteller (1987): BSA = √(height × weight ÷ 3600) — simple, and the formula most commonly used in clinical practice today, including chemotherapy dosing. Du Bois (1916): BSA = 0.007184 × height0.725 × weight0.425 — the classic formula, still widely cited. The two usually agree within a few percent. For reference, an average adult BSA is around 1.7–1.9 m². Never use this calculator to set a medication dose yourself — dosing is a clinician's job, and many drugs adjust further for organ function and other factors.
