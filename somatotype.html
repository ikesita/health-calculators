Ectomorphy here uses the published Heath-Carter height–weight ratio equation; endomorphy and mesomorphy use simple estimates from waist-to-height ratio and wrist size relative to height — reasonable tendencies, not measurements.

Common questions

What are the three somatotypes?
Endomorphy (roundness), mesomorphy (muscularity/frame) and ectomorphy (linearity). Everyone has all three in some proportion.

How accurate is this estimate?
Rough only. The formal Heath-Carter method uses ten measurements including skinfolds and bone breadths. Use this page for a general tendency.

Can your somatotype change?
Your frame is fixed, but fat and muscle are not — training and diet can shift the endomorphy and mesomorphy components over time.
